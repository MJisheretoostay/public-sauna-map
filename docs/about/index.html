About Public Sauna Map

Public Sauna Map is a small, independent directory of public saunas in the United States: places where an individual can book a seat or walk in, such as community wood-fired saunas, Russian and Eastern European banyas, communal bathhouses and sauna-and-cold-plunge clubs.

How it's made

To be upfront: this site is built and maintained with the help of AI tools. Listings are researched from each venue's own website (sometimes supplemented by a reputable local guide), and every listing links to its sources and shows the date it was last checked. We do not visit every venue in person. If we can't confirm a detail, we mark it Unknown.

What we don't do

  • We don't make health or medical claims. Talk to a doctor if you have questions about whether sauna or cold water is right for you.
  • Venues don't pay to be listed, and listings aren't ranked by payment.

Money

The site may eventually earn money through affiliate links in home sauna and cold plunge buying guides. See the affiliate disclosure. As of today, no affiliate links are active.

Corrections

Venues change prices, hours and locations often. If something is wrong, we want to fix it. A contact address is coming soon.
