Public saunas in Houston, TX

Houston's public sauna options are spread across the metro: Saunawelt in Webster/Clear Lake is a European-style communal sauna house with Finnish and herbal saunas and scheduled Aufguss, while two large Korean spas (jjimjilbang) on the west side, Gangnam Spa and Spa World in Katy, offer all-day walk-in admission with wood-fired kiln saunas and gender-separated hot and cold baths. Saunawelt is book-ahead only; the Korean spas take walk-ins.

3 venues, each checked against the venue's own website on 2026-11-20. Fields we could not confirm are marked Unknown. Prices and hours change, so confirm before you go.

Schedule / who
Hours as listed: Mon-Fri 10am-8:30pm, Sat 9am-8:30pm, Sun 11am-8:30pm. Advance booking required (same-day bookings when space allows). Notes
Communal sauna house (soft-opened April 2026) with a hemlock Finnish sauna where water is poured on the stones, a Brechelbad Alpine herbal sauna, an aromatherapy steam room, infrared loungers and indoor and creek-side outdoor lounges with herbal tea. Aufguss, infusions and breathwork are on the Programme Calendar. A towel and sauna seat cover are provided; bring swimwear, non-slip slides and a glass-free water bottle. Cashless; phones stay in lockers. Multipasses from $145 (3 Ritual visits).

Each bath area has a hot tub (typically 103-109°F), whirlpool, jet pool, cold pool, dry sauna and herbal steam room. Small towels provided, large towels $2. Phones must stay in lockers in the locker rooms and bath areas.
