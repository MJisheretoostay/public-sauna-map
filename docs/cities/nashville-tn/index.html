Public saunas in Nashville, TN

Nashville's public sauna scene is a cluster of social sauna and cold plunge studios, all within about two miles of downtown: Framework's indoor-outdoor flagship in Wedgewood-Houston and its open-air Backyard in East Nashville, Lolu's 40-person sauna and saltwater cold pool on Main Street, and Ritual's community sauna on Porter Road. None of the four states its heat source, and no public wood-fired sauna was found. The many infrared-only studios in town, and private-room bathhouses such as Holiday Salon & Bathhouse, are not listed.

4 venues, each checked against the venue's own website on 2026-11-26. Fields we could not confirm are marked Unknown. Prices and hours change, so confirm before you go.
