Public saunas in Duluth, MN

Duluth has a long community sauna tradition, and today's public options include a 1920s-era steam bath downtown, a harborside Nordic sauna with a floating sauna and Lake Superior cold plunge at Pier B, and a mobile wood-fired sauna that sets up community sessions around town. Hours and locations change by season, so confirm with each venue before you go.

Cedar & Stone Nordic Sauna (Pier B flagship)

Canal Park / Duluth harbor · 800 W Railroad St, Duluth, MN 55802
Wood-firedElectricFloatingOutdoor
Drop-in price
$49 per guest for Open Sauna (walk-ups welcome, no reservation) or a Social Sauna session; $30 local rate via email promo code; private sessions from $79 per guest
Session length
At least 90 minutes for Open Sauna; 75 minutes for Social Sauna and social floating sauna
Cold plunge
Harborside natural cold plunge in Lake Superior (included, optional)
Schedule / who
Thursday-Monday 9am-9pm. Open Sauna sessions start every 45 minutes from 2:45pm to 8:45pm. Social sessions are shared with up to 10 guests; all other session types are reserved in advance.
Notes
Three saunas on site: a wood-fired social sauna, an electric private sauna and a wood-fired floating sauna. Guided sessions with tea, towels and outdoor cold showers. Bring a swimsuit and footwear for the plunge in freezing weather. Also offers a $25 monthly community cold plunge (second Sunday). The venue's page lists $49 for Open Sauna; third-party booking sites show different totals with fees.
