Public saunas in Seattle, WA

Seattle's public sauna scene is mostly mobile and on the water: wood-fired saunas parked at Alki Beach, Golden Gardens and Lincoln Park, where the cold plunge is Puget Sound, plus a floating sauna on Lake Union. Park-based saunas depend on permits, so locations can shift.

5 venues, each checked against the venue's own website on 2026-10-21. Fields we could not confirm are marked Unknown. Prices and hours change, so confirm before you go.
