Public saunas in Austin, TX

Austin's public sauna scene is made up of drop-in social bathhouses and small sauna gardens, mostly in East Austin, South Lamar and North Loop. Ceremony Spa and Bathe run communal bathhouses with saunas and cold plunges, Sauna House runs a wood-burning Sauna Garden, and Cøntrast offers a walk-in garden sauna with two cold plunges. Prices, hours and session formats change, so confirm with each venue before you go.

4 venues, each checked against the venue's own website on 2026-11-05. Fields we could not confirm are marked Unknown. Prices and hours change, so confirm before you go.
