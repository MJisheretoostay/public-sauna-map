Public saunas in Detroit, MI

Detroit pairs a bathhouse that has been open to guests since 1930 with a newer wave of wood-fired and Finnish-style saunas. The Schvitz in the North End takes walk-ins on a weekly schedule of all-gender, men-only and women-only hours, BASK in Brush Park sells two-hour sauna and cold plunge sessions, and two outdoor operations, Islandview Sauna Club and SOMi Sauna on Belle Isle, run wood-fired saunas on weekends. Prices, hours and seasonal dates change, so confirm with each venue before you go.

Saturday evenings after 6pm are reserved for private parties.
