Public saunas in Chicago, IL

Chicago's public sauna scene is anchored by old-school Russian-style bathhouses: Chicago Bath House in Wicker Park says it has operated since 1906, and Chicago Sweatlodge on the Northwest Side is a men-only banya with a restaurant. Newer options include a Finnish sauna and plunge club in Fulton Market, a thermal-bath circuit in River West, and a wood-fired sauna on Oak Street Beach that uses Lake Michigan water for its plunge. The bathhouses take walk-ins; most newer spots sell timed sessions, so book ahead.

5 venues, each checked against the venue's own website on 2026-10-29. Fields we could not confirm are marked Unknown. Prices and hours change, so confirm before you go.
