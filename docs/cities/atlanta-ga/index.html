Public saunas in Atlanta, GA

Atlanta's public sauna scene is centered on the Korean spas (jjimjilbang) of Gwinnett County and the Buford Highway corridor, roughly 15-25 miles northeast of downtown. Jeju Sauna and Spa Land in Duluth, Gangnam Sauna in Norcross and Roman Holiday Sauna in Doraville all sell walk-in day admission covering mineral-stone sauna rooms, steam and gender-separated bath areas, and three of the four are open around the clock. A communal Nordic-style sauna club for intown Atlanta (Atlanta Sauna Club) has announced plans for 2027 but has no site yet.

4 venues, each checked against the venue's own website on 2026-11-23. Fields we could not confirm are marked Unknown. Prices and hours change, so confirm before you go.

Saunas and bathhouse open 24/7 including all holidays; other services have shorter hours. No appointments, first come first served. Gender-separated bathing areas require full nudity; spa uniform provided for shared areas. Barefoot facility.

Large Korean bathhouse with natural mineral-stone sauna rooms including salt and rock rooms, steam rooms, traditional baths, a heated jade-stone pool, a junior Olympic swimming pool (bring your own swimsuit; none provided) and a fitness room. A physical government-issued photo ID is required (paper copies refused); a $100 cash deposit is accepted instead. Food, scrubs and massages are charged to the wristband and paid at checkout; complimentary toiletries.
