Public saunas in Portland, OR

Portland's public sauna scene mixes riverside options with indoor sauna houses: a mobile wood-fired sauna on the Willamette, a floating sauna on the Columbia, and a wood-fired social sauna behind a SE Belmont bar. Indoor choices include two Finnish-style sauna houses and a rooftop pool-and-sauna bathhouse downtown. Most places sell timed sessions, so book ahead.

6 venues, each checked against the venue's own website on 2026-10-28. Fields we could not confirm are marked Unknown. Prices and hours change, so confirm before you go.
