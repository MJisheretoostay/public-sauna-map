Public saunas in New York, NY

New York's public sauna scene runs from the walk-in Russian & Turkish Baths in the East Village, which says it has served the city since 1892, to modern bathhouses with banyas, Finnish-style saunas and cold pools in Manhattan and Brooklyn. Brooklyn's World Spa has a large banya and many heat rooms, and QC NY puts saunas and outdoor heated pools on Governors Island. Most newer venues sell timed day passes online, so book ahead, while the Russian & Turkish Baths only takes walk-ins.

8 venues, each checked against the venue's own website on 2026-10-30. Fields we could not confirm are marked Unknown. Prices and hours change, so confirm before you go.
