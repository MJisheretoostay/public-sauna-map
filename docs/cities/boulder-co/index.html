Public saunas in Boulder, CO

Boulder's public sauna scene is made up of small independent bathhouses and sauna clubs, most of them clustered in the North Boulder Arts District along Broadway, plus a converted warehouse bathhouse in east Boulder and a garden sauna club near downtown on Pearl Street. Ever Sky, Third Space and Berner Collective all sell single drop-in visits around $30-35, while PORTAL runs bookable sessions on a credit system. Prices, hours and session formats change, so confirm with each venue before you go.
