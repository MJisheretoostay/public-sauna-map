Public saunas in Pittsburgh, PA

Pittsburgh's public sauna scene mixes outdoor wood-fired pop-ups in the East End with an indoor social sauna studio in the Strip District. Bad Sauna runs hourly open, silent and aufguss sessions at Ascend Point Breeze, Signal Sauna runs a wood-fired barrel sauna with ice-cold tubs on a lot off Torrens Street, and MindFuel Method offers open flow and guided sessions in a large cedar sauna with cold plunge tubs. All three are book-ahead, so check each venue's schedule before you go.

Hours as listed: Tue 3-10pm, Wed-Thu 5-10pm, Fri 3-11pm, Sat 10am-8pm, Sun 10am-10pm. Open, Silent, Aufguss and themed nights (including Women's Night and Trans Night) on the booking calendar. 18+, clothing-optional inside the sauna, phone-free.

Founded in 2023 by a certified bath master. Schedule / who
Weekly schedule lists Open Flow sessions Wed-Thu 4-8:15pm, Sat 11:30am-6:45pm, Sun 10am-6:45pm (Sunday 1pm is a Queer Session); closed Mon, Tue and Fri. Book online or in the FitDEGREE app.
Notes
Mobile pop-up on a converted lot: a wood-fired barrel sauna (typically 150-190°F; you can pour water on the rocks) with outdoor changing rooms and a portable toilet. Entrance on Torrens St behind the big pink building; street parking on Hamilton Ave. Bring two towels, clothes you don't mind getting wet and a water bottle. Memberships from $80/month; 5- and 10-session passes $110 and $200.
