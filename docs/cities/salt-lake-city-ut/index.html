Public saunas in Salt Lake City, UT

Salt Lake City's public sauna scene is a mix of Finnish-style walk-in saunas, a full bathhouse and hosted Nordic sessions, spread from downtown and Poplar Grove to Sugar House and South Salt Lake. Sauna Public, Löyly Nordic Spa and Cold Plunge Coffee take walk-ins with no time limit, while Glow SLC sells 2-hour bathhouse visits and PLUNJ runs hosted 60-minute sessions. Prices, hours and session formats change, so confirm with each venue before you go.

5 venues, each checked against the venue's own website on 2026-11-09. Fields we could not confirm are marked Unknown. Prices and hours change, so confirm before you go.
