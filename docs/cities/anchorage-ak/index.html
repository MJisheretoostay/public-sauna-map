Public saunas in Anchorage, AK

Anchorage has few dedicated public saunas, but it does have two Nordic-style spas with hot and cold circuits and a municipal recreation center with dry saunas in its locker rooms. The Alaska Club's Nordic Spa sells 90-minute sessions to nonmembers in East Anchorage, Alyeska Nordic Spa in Girdwood (part of the Municipality of Anchorage, about 40 miles south of downtown) sells full-day passes, and Spenard Recreation Center lets visitors use its saunas after checking in at the front desk. Prices, hours and access rules change, so confirm with each venue before you go.
