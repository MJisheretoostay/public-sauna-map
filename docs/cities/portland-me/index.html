Public saunas in Portland, ME

Portland's only in-town public bathhouse is Washington Baths on the East End, a walk-in-only spot with gender-separate saunas and a shared courtyard with hot and cold pools. The rest of the area's drop-in options are small wood-fired community saunas and a campground Nordic spa within about 20-25 minutes of downtown, in Scarborough and Freeport, where you book a seat in a shared session. Several mobile operators work the southern Maine coast seasonally; Village Sauna in South Portland was temporarily closed as of 2026-11-24 while it looks for a winter site.

4 venues, each checked against the venue's own website on 2026-11-24. Fields we could not confirm are marked Unknown. Prices and hours change, so confirm before you go.

Washington Baths

East End (Washington Avenue) · 145 Washington Ave, Portland, ME 04101
BathhouseWood-fired
Drop-in price
$38 single entry; 5-pass card $165; 10-pass card $310
Session length
No time limit (venue says a typical visit lasts 1.5-2 hours)
Cold plunge
Outdoor cold pool (50F) in a shared courtyard, plus a 104F hot pool
Schedule / who
Thursday-Monday 12pm-9pm, last entry 8pm. Walk-in only, no reservations; in-person waitlist when full. Silence observed on Fridays. Gender-separate, clothing-optional sauna rooms; bathing suits required in common areas. Monthly Queer Community Day with all-gender bathing. Closed Nov 26-27 (Thanksgiving) and Dec 22-25, 2026.
Notes
Saunas use a traditional thermal-mass stove (kiuas) that can be fired with wood or gas; water may be poured on the rocks. Venue lists sauna air at roughly 180-200F. 44 lockers, lounge and library, small cafe; no outside food or drink. Bring a swimsuit, towel and sandals (towel rental $3, free sandals). Groups are discouraged; keep groups to 5 or fewer.
