Public saunas in Dallas, TX

Dallas-Fort Worth's public heat is spread across the northern metro: King Spa & Sauna, a Korean jjimjilbang on Royal Lane in Dallas; Spa Castle Texas and the Russian Banya of Dallas, both in Carrollton; and WorldSprings, an outdoor mineral-pool park with seven saunas and daily Aufguss sessions in The Colony. The Russian Banya is the only venue here that says its banya is heated by a wood-burning stove. Recovery lounges that mix saunas with red-light and compression devices, and spa-club saunas that have not opened yet, are not listed.

4 venues, each checked against the venue's own website on 2026-11-27. Fields we could not confirm are marked Unknown. Prices and hours change, so confirm before you go.

Scrubs and massages cost extra; screen golf and karaoke rooms are on site.
